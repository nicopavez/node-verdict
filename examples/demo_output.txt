3. Four repair policies on the same pool

naive: replace any rank slower than the job median, mid-run.
naive+checkpoint: the same, but wait for a checkpoint. Any policy can do this.
peer-aware: replace a rank persistently slow against its stage peers, at a checkpoint. The fair baseline.
attributed: verdict engine plus policy.

Nearly all of it is checkpoint timing and comparing to stage peers. Attribution adds the chip, an owner for every slow job, and no replacement on one job of evidence. Pulling the chip costs a restart, and this model does not price corrupted training, so attribution scores slightly below peer-aware.
